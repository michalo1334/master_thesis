Probabilities are model inputs, not empirical exploit rates.
- Defenses include CVSS-prioritized patching, random selection, topology/policy segmentation, simulation-informed selection, and annealing search. Unit action cost means budgets compare action counts, not real-world cost.

## Evaluation design, status, evidence
- Study design in thesis foundation `docs/foundation/05-azure-evaluation-method.md`: three frozen topology tiers (small/medium/large); four alternatives against CVSS, budgets 1/2/3; 36 primary mission-impact comparisons. Paired attack seeds. Primary estimand is paired mean difference in simulated mission impact. Blast radius is secondary; modeled pre-attack mission disruption is a separate feasibility outcome.
- Two uncertainty sources: plan-selection and attack outcomes. Pilot selects common sample counts against a precision target; final data use disjoint seeds. Holm correction covers 36 primary comparisons. One graph per tier means graph-generation variation is not represented. Runtime replicas/warm-up are separate from attack evidence.
- Important status distinction: the thesis drafts explicitly state no full-study empirical results. Approved Azure method/tooling and scale-tier inputs remain incomplete; the README roadmap lists cloud runner, diagnostics, environment records, warm-up, Azure study as future work. Local pilots validate workflow/select parameters only, not final strategy claims. Existing scenario inputs include rehearsal and optimizer pilot cases and fixed-order fulfillment/NVD snapshot; do not present these as final evidence without provenance checks.
- Evidence chain documented in `docs/foundation/01-evidence-provenance.md`: frozen manifest/graph → evaluation archive (plans, trials, outcomes, runtimes/checksums) → semantic replica checks + statistical analysis + environment record → accepted package → bounded claims. Literature supports external claims; project docs do not replace publications.
- Evaluation limitations: fixed stylized attacker/topology; synthetic or reviewed scenario data do not establish real exploit rates; no broad network/attacker generalization; unit-cost actions do not represent practical costs. Study tiers and controlled scale study may be a planned design rather than completed inputs.

## Facts versus user decisions
Facts available in repositories: model behavior and its limits; implementation status; draft chapter structure; planned study protocol; evidence artifacts/status; local template/build requirements; visible placeholders and known validity risks.

Only user/supervisor/institution can resolve: thesis title and final research question/contribution; target language and required abstract/summary; whether the Azure study will be completed, scope/cutoff, and what to do if blocked; thesis deadline, milestones, weekly availability, and review cadence; required length/format and current institutional rules; supervisor priorities and accepted evidence standard; whether scope is implementation + evaluation or a narrower contribution; access/budget/permissions for Azure and data; final claims based on results not yet collected.

## Planning and grilling implications
- First establish deadline, available hours, desired language, and supervisor/institution constraints; then establish evidence feasibility and a stop/go date for Azure. These decisions control whether the plan targets a measured study or a carefully bounded prototype/method thesis.
- Build milestones around decisions and evidence gates: research question/claim matrix and literature method; reconcile code/draft; freeze evaluation inputs; pilot/tooling; final collection and analysis (conditional); write/revise chapters; references, front matter, template validation, supervisor review, submission buffer.
- Do not schedule unsupported results as certain deliverables. Define a fallback narrative if cloud evaluation cannot be completed, and reserve time for evidence audit, citations, and compile/layout fixes.
- Track each planned claim to source: implementation fact → code/docs; empirical claim → accepted archive/analysis/environment; literature claim → cited publication. Keep plan-selection vs attack seeds and simulated vs real-world claims distinct.
