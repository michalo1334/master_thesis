# Overview

The most important part of any claim is to test it scientifically with reproducible results. This reference provides a
**non-exhausting** list of heuristics and tools that can be utilized for performing observational or experimental tests.

The choice of testing methods is dependent on the complexity of the claim and completeness. More thorough ones provide  
better confidence in the empirical measurements, at the cost of complexity and/or time.

Always start with simplest method, determine if it's sufficient to claim the hypothesis is proven, rejected or  
plausible, then optionally bring out the "bigger guns".
